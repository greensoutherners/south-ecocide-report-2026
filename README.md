# Ecocide, Urbicide and Ethnocide in South Lebanon

Green Southerners' research report on the 2026 war in South Lebanon, 1 March to
14 October 2026, by the Green Southerners team.

- **Full report**: [index.html](https://greensoutherners.github.io/south-ecocide-report-2026/)
  and its PDF, `green-southerners-south-lebanon-2026-report-draft.pdf`
- **Short edition**: [short.html](https://greensoutherners.github.io/south-ecocide-report-2026/short.html)
  and its PDF, `green-southerners-south-lebanon-2026-report-short-draft.pdf`

**Status: draft, under review.** Figures and wording may change before the final
edition. When it is final, this repository will hold that edition as it was
published.

The report publishes statistics, never records: counts, sums and shares by week,
month and town, each category of record counted apart. The figures, maps and
platform views come from the South Lebanon corpus and its public site,
[South Ecocide Analytics](https://greensoutherners.github.io/south-ecocide-analytics/).
The records themselves and the Visual Archive of Attacks and Damage are
available on request: write to info@greensoutherners.org, introducing yourself
and stating the purpose for which you need the data.
